About Our Vehicle Services

At Idec Shipping and Trading (PVT) Ltd, we specialize in providing seamless and efficient vehicle clearance solutions for imports and exports. Our experienced team ensures a hassle-free process by managing all documentation, customs compliance, and regulatory requirements on your behalf.

We understand the complexities of vehicle logistics, which is why we offer end-to-end services — from port clearance and duty payments to storage and final delivery. Whether you're importing a single car or managing a fleet, we guarantee timely and cost-effective clearance.

Customs clearance for vehicles requires approval from national authorities, confirming that all taxes and duties are paid and the vehicle meets local standards. As your trusted agent, we secure this authorization swiftly, keeping your shipment moving without delays.
