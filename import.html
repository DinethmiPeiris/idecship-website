About Our Import Services

At IDEC Shipping and Trading (Pvt) Ltd, we make importing into Sri Lanka straightforward, stress-free, and fully compliant. Our experienced team manages every aspect of the import process — from origin documentation and freight booking to customs clearance and final delivery — so you can focus entirely on your business.

With deep expertise in Sri Lankan customs regulations and strong relationships with global shipping lines, we handle shipments of all sizes and commodity types. Whether you are importing raw materials, consumer goods or machinery, we provide tailored solutions designed to minimize delays and reduce overall landed costs.

Our end-to-end import management covers LCL and FCL ocean freight, air express cargo, customs brokerage, regulatory compliance, and inland transportation. Every shipment is handled with the highest level of care, accuracy, and transparency from start to finish. Trust IDEC to keep your supply chain moving.
