About BOI Logistics

The Board of Investment (BOI) of Sri Lanka is the apex government body responsible for promoting and facilitating investment in the country. BOI-approved enterprises enjoy a range of privileges including duty exemptions, tax concessions, and streamlined customs procedures — but navigating these benefits requires expert knowledge of complex regulatory frameworks.

At IDEC Shipping and Trading (Pvt) Ltd, we serve as the dedicated logistics partner for BOI-approved companies operating across Sri Lanka. Our experienced team understands the intricacies of BOI regulations, enabling us to process duty-free imports, manage bonded warehouse operations, and ensure full compliance with BOI permit conditions on your behalf.

Whether you are a manufacturing enterprise importing raw materials, an export processing zone company managing inbound components, or a BOI project requiring specialist freight solutions, IDEC provides end-to-end logistics support tailored specifically to the BOI regulatory environment. We keep your supply chain compliant, efficient, and cost-effective.

BOI Services

Duty Exemption Clearance

Expert processing of duty-free import clearances under BOI permits. We handle all documentation, entitlement verifications, and Customs Department coordination to secure your exemptions without delay.

BOI Permit Management

Full lifecycle management of BOI import and export permits — from initial application through renewal and amendments. We liaise directly with BOI authorities to keep your permits current and valid.

Bonded Warehouse Operations

Management of bonded storage for goods under BOI duty suspension. Our facilities allow your inventory to be stored, processed, and released in strict accordance with BOI bonding conditions.

Raw Material Imports

Specialized import handling for manufacturing raw materials, components, and machinery under BOI duty concession schemes, ensuring uninterrupted supply to your production lines.

Export Zone Freight

Dedicated freight solutions for Export Processing Zone (EPZ) enterprises, covering inbound component logistics, re-export clearances, and full BOI compliance documentation.

Regulatory Compliance

Ongoing compliance advisory covering BOI condition monitoring, audit preparation, and coordination with government authorities including Customs, SLSI, and the Ministry of Finance.
