Our Story

Built on trust,
driven by excellence

IDEC Shipping and Trading (Pvt) Ltd was founded with a single, clear purpose — to remove the complexity from international trade and give businesses of every size access to world-class logistics. Headquartered in the heart of Colombo, we have grown into one of Sri Lanka's most trusted freight forwarding and customs clearance specialists.

Over the years, we have forged strong partnerships with global shipping lines, airlines, and government authorities, enabling us to offer our clients unparalleled access to international trade networks. Our deep roots in Colombo's trade community give us a distinct advantage in navigating Sri Lanka's regulatory environment with speed and precision.
