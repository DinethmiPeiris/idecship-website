About Our Export Services

In an increasingly connected global economy, efficient export operations are vital for business growth and market expansion. At IDEC Shipping and Trading (Pvt) Ltd, we serve as your trusted gateway to international markets, providing comprehensive export solutions tailored to your business needs.

With extensive expertise in global trade practices and Sri Lankan export regulations, we streamline cross-border shipments for enterprises of all scales. Whether you're exporting agricultural products, manufactured goods, or specialized commodities, we ensure your cargo reaches its destination securely, compliantly, and within the required timelines.

We combine time-tested trade wisdom with cutting-edge logistics strategies to create customized solutions for every export challenge. From initial documentation to final loading and transport, our experienced team maintains rigorous standards at every step of the export journey.

At IDEC, we go beyond mere logistics — we foster lasting business relationships by offering dependable, transparent, and stress-free export services. Entrust us with your international shipments while you concentrate on building your global presence.

FCL

Priority air cargo services for time-critical exports, connecting Sri Lanka to major international hubs with speed and precision.

Freight Handling

End-to-end handling of export cargo from warehouse to port, ensuring safe, efficient loading and documentation compliance.

Packing
